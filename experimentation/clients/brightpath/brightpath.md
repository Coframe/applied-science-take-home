# BrightPath

Mid-market e-learning platform. Sells individual course enrolments at **$49 each**
(it also offers annual team licences, but the pages below convert on single-course
purchases, so $49 is the value of one conversion). Traffic is spread across the
homepage and individual course landing pages — there are hundreds of courses, but
the top 5 drive the majority of traffic, leaving a long tail of low-traffic pages.

BrightPath wants a **monthly** readout.

Structured inputs: see `config.json`.
