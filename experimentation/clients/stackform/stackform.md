# Stackform

B2B developer tooling, mid-market. Sells to engineering teams through a demo-led
motion. Average contract value is $4,200; roughly 14% of demos close, with a
typical sales cycle of about 75 days.

The demo request page's `revenue_per_conversion` is the **expected value of one
demo**: `$4,200 × 14% ≈ $588`. Because of the ~75-day sales cycle, that revenue
lands well after the conversion.

Stackform wants a **monthly** readout.

Structured inputs: see `config.json`.
