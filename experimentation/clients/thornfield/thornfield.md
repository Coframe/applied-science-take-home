# Thornfield & Co

Mid-market apparel retailer, ~$40M annual online revenue. Sells across the US and
Canada. The main drop-off is between the product page and checkout.

Only the checkout step carries an order value (`revenue_per_conversion = $185`);
the homepage and product page are upstream funnel steps.

Thornfield wants a **bi-weekly** (every 14 days) readout.

Structured inputs: see `config.json`.
